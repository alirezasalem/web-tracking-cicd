# Feature Brief FB-005: Remove from Cart Tracking

## Background
Users frequently add items to their cart and then remove them before checkout.
Product wants to understand which items get removed and at what stage.

## What we want to track
When a user removes an item from their cart, we need to know:
- Which item was removed (SKU, name, price, quantity)
- The cart's value before and after removal
- Whether the user proceeded to checkout afterwards or abandoned

## Event details
- Event name: `remove_from_cart`
- Fires on: click of the "Remove" button on cart line items
- Page: `/cart`
- Selector: `[data-testid="remove-item-btn"]`

## Business rules
- Must include full `items` array (GA4 ecommerce standard)
- Must include `currency` and `value` of the removed item
- Should NOT fire on quantity decreases — only full removals
- Should fire once per click (no duplicate pushes)

## Acceptance
- Event appears in GA4 DebugView within 5 seconds of clicking remove
- All required ecommerce parameters present
- No firing on quantity change to non-zero values
